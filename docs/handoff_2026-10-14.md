## Lab lessons from today

- **sierra-hw's `ref` needs a full commit hash or a branch name.** A short hash fails at checkout (five runs lost ~15 minutes).
- **`abreport.py` with two results folders needs named sides**: `NAME=log,log`.
- **The object-report log line is cut at 512 bytes** on busy maps (a50, b30's beach). Sleep counts read from it there are incomplete.
- **b30's flight in the cache models can't judge exactness between two builds** with different code layout: they diverge after ~1,050–1,460 ticks. Use d20, a50 and c20.

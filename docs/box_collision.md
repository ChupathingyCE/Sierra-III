# Box: the collision service (bottleneck, 2026-11-11; v0, spec started)

The lab's `projects/sierra/research/opportunities/REBUILD.md` is the spine (§3.3: "with biped motion"; §7 step 4). The design draft behind this page is the lab's `projects/sierra/research/opportunities/collision.md` (DeepSeek's draft; its [M] rows checked by research 2026-11-11 12:5x, b30's ms corrected; its [U] lines and ceilings stay leads) with its callers' anatomy, `collision_anatomy_ds.md`. This page doesn't restate them: it sets the box's contract, budget and shape, and carries their labels as they are. Labels: **[M]** measured on a PSP, **[E]** emulator or a count, **[D]** read in code or docs, **[U]** inference.

**What it is:** every collision query the tick makes, through one service with its own data: sphere queries (the features in a sphere: the level's surfaces, edges and vertices, and the objects' bounds), vector tests (rays: first hit), and point tests (which leaf and cluster a point is in). Its askers are other boxes: biped motion first (the pill sweep), vehicles and mass points, projectiles, point physics, the AI's lines of fire and sight, and the drawing's rays.

**State:** spec started (2026-11-11).

## 1. Contract

- **Reads:** the level's collision BSP and its surfaces (the tags, read only, laid out the service's way), the objects' bounds and their cluster membership (the world store: posing's sphere section, and the walk's map links), each query's arguments.
- **Writes:** the answers only (a features list, a hit, a leaf), into the asker's own memory. Today's visit marks on surfaces and objects become per-query state, so a query writes nothing shared and can run on either core.
- **Who asks, and when** [D, E: `collision_anatomy_ds.md` §1]: biped motion's pill sweep per moving biped per tick (~90% of the level sphere walks on b30, 36 a tick), the crouch and stand test (player bipeds only), mass points' ground plane (vehicles), vehicle suspension and effect markers, projectiles' lines, point physics (some on the ME already), the AI's line of fire and sight (the ME's twin serves part), and in the drawing sound obstruction, lens flares and object lighting.

The level never moves within a tick except breakable surfaces, whose flags are part of its state.

## 2. Data

- **The level:** node records with their plane inline (the node and its plane are read together every step, from two arrays today), surfaces with their edges and vertices packed, the top nodes in the scratchpad as today (`collision.md` §2's data layout) [D, U].
- **The objects:** the bounds the queries test, in arrays by cluster (structure of arrays: a query reads one sphere of many objects), patched on the reconnect that changes them, not rebuilt per query.
- **What counting says about it** [E, research's §1a in `collision.md`; the lab's dcanat1 42989839989, dcanat2 `footprint.md`]: collision's functions take **8,990 data misses a tick on b30** (28% of the tick's, the largest system) and 2,802 on c20 (21%). The sphere table and cluster references are already compact (137 lines, 2,119 fills a tick on b30): they miss because they're **evicted**, not because they're spread. So packing alone doesn't pay (CLUSTERPACK, rejected neutral on the PSP-3000; the C2 premise "51 → 16 lines" doesn't hold); what pays is a **smaller working set over the tick**, or the queries' misses moved where they don't stall the tick.

## 3. Placement

- **The main CPU** for the serial form. The VFPU only for 4-wide plane tests, and last: a plane test is one dot product and two compares a node, chasing tag data (`hardware_utilization.md`).
- **Code:** today the eight sampled functions are 23,976 B, 1.5x the 16 KB I-cache, and the port ships two walk implementations chosen per walk by its leaves (`collision_bsp.c`), so the resident set differs by section [D]. The service is one path, its target ~10-12 KB [U] (`collision.md` §2), written so the engine's walk's speed on c20's one- and two-leaf walks is kept: ONEWALK (the port's walk always) was exact and **slower on c20** on the PSP-3000 (tick +1.2%) [M].
- **The second core:** the parallel form's queries (askers at rest, far bipeds, level-only rays) can run on the ME in the parallel phase; the ME's room is the tick schedule's to give (at 15 fps the snowfield has ~13 ms a tick free, the beach ~9 [M, `me_room_fights_*`]).

- **Code budget (REBUILD §3.6): fits.** The walk's own functions run **99-248 lines a tick** on their own cache pair [E, the lab's findings `fpass2_own_pair_by_subsystem`: `collision_test_vector` 99-146 lines, ~0-14 I-misses a tick; `collision_bsp_test_sphere` 84-102 lines, 0 I-misses]. That's one stage under the I-cache's 256 lines, so its code stays resident; its wait is data. The ~24 KB above is the sampled set across both walk implementations, not one walk's lines.

## 4. Budget

- **Today** [M shares, PSP-1000, tock162 b3461c1's shipping sampler; ms U on M]: collision is 13.3-18.4% of the tick (c20 14.0, a30 14.2, b30 18.4, b40 13.3): **~2.9 ms on c20, ~3.4 on a30, ~4.8-5.6 on b30's beach play, ~10 on b30's flight** (25.6% of a ~39 ms tick, the lab's s_gap §2), **~5.0 on b40's snowfield** (13.3% of 37.6 [M, b151 ledger]); the drawing's rays another 0.7-1.2 ms a frame.
- **v0: 2.2 ms a tick on the beach and the snowfield** for every asker, inside biped motion's 4.5 (`box_biped_motion.md` §4), proportional to the snowfield's split [U]. Per query: b30 makes ~88 collision calls a tick (42 `collision_test_vector` + 46 `collision_bsp_test_sphere`, `cpu_cost_map.md` §4a [E]), so **~25 µs a query** against ~57 today [U]. (2026-11-15: counted by asker instead, at the askers' own calls, b30_leg1 makes **~144 a tick**, ~71 in the tick, ~61 in the ME's jobs and ~20 a frame in the drawing (§1, the askers, counted): the 88 counted two of the entries, in the tick alone, and the per-query figure stands on that count.)
- **So the cut is about 2.5x on the snowfield and the beach, and 4.5x on the flight.** The sized pieces reach far less: collision's one stage 0.54-0.75 ms [E], the object half packed (CSPK) whose premise the counts undid, the rest under any floor (`collision.md` §5's slice states). **The rest is unsized and is this box's question.**

## 5. Means

- **A (a smaller working set):** one walk instead of two, node and plane in one record, surfaces packed, and per-query state instead of marks written into shared data. Worth: unsized; the data side's 8,990 misses a tick on b30 are 1.9-3.8 ms at the PSP's miss price [U on E] (`collision.md` O1).
- **B (work on change):** what repeats is askers at rest, not rays [E]: biped level spheres repeat 12-20% on b30, resting bipeds' model spheres 84.4% on a30. Exact reuse of the level walk's answer was tried and bought nothing (S2REUSE: 26-33% reused, neutral on the PSP-3000, because the level walk is cheap there [M]). So B skips the whole asker's query, level and objects, when nothing in its reach changed since its last answer: the service's cluster arrays know which objects in reach moved, the guard STILLSWEEP lacked ("objects in reach had moved"). It's "plays the same" for an asker whose inputs and reach are unchanged and exact when the guard is complete; proven by play and seeds where exact, the outcome census where not.
- **C (both cores):** queries only read once marks are per-query, so the parallel form's queries run on either core against last tick's objects (the hazard's reach, `box_biped_motion.md` Open 1: half of a biped's reads of other bipeds land on ones updated earlier this tick).

## 6. What it replaces

`collision_get_features_in_sphere`, `collision_bsp_test_sphere` and both sphere walks (the engine's `bsp3d/bsp2d_test_sphere_recursive` and the port's `psp_bsp3d/2d_test_sphere`), `collision_test_vector` and `collision_bsp_test_vector_recursive`, `collision_features_test_vector`, `bsp3d_test_point` as the tick uses it, the objects' half (`objects_in_sphere`, the cluster walks), and the port's answer memos (BSPR's standing answers stay until B replaces them). **Earlier tries** (the lab's `projects/sierra/tools/levercheck.py --ground collision`, 2026-11-11; `collision.md` §4 says why each does or doesn't sink this): BP and P2NC (on), ONEWALK (slower on c20), CLUSTERPACK (neutral), S2REUSE, SMEMO, BSPR, COLLMEMO and BSPW (answer reuse and faster walks: neutral or noise), CSPK, PACKFEAT, SC1 (sized, unbuilt), CT and RT1 (covered by CW), STILLSWEEP (stopped on its guard: reopens under B), VFPUMATH, BETWEENRAYS, DRAWRAYSME, BW and FARHALF (the far tier: shelved slower on b30's flight).

**Upstream manifest:** the collision and BSP functions and the level's collision tags at the pin after the rebase (REBUILD §5b).

## 7. Proof

Alone, against a capture of its queries over a pad bench (each query's arguments, the level, the objects' bounds that tick, the engine's answer): the service's answers equal the engine's (the serial form is exact: same features, same order, same hit). In the frame: play and both seeds identical for the serial form; for B's skips and the parallel form, play and the outcome census, and the multiplayer boundary (the client's own biped well inside the host's 3.5 units; hit reports true).

## 11. The ME's half of the pill sweep (2026-11-19)

**On by default since 2026-11-20** (the lead: exact, real-faster on b30's flight, no worse anywhere measured; the tick schedule's slice 1 with it): `PSP_SWEEPME` and `PSP_BIPEDSTAGE` built in the campaign build, keep word `sweepme` on (off: `sweepmeoff`), ME list `rbtsw` (`docs/build_switches.md`).

The lead's order of 2026-11-19 11:5xZ (the lab's coord/inbox/builder.md), on line (b)'s falsifier: from eDRAM the ME runs the sweep at x0.86-0.94 of the main CPU [M, PSP-1000, the lab's `sweepme_falsifier_beach`, `sweepme_falsifier_flight`; §10 is on Sierra-III builder/boxme until the two branches merge]. Code: `psp/src/psp_sweepme.c`, `psp/include/psp_sweepme.h`, the game's builds of the box (`psp/src/box_collbsp_game.c`, `box_sweep_game.c`), `psp_fixups.py` FIXES_SWEEPME, the MEXEC list `psp/layout/mexec_rbsw.txt`. Build switch `PSP_SWEEPME=1`, keep word `sweepme` (off by default), `sweepmecheck` its check.

**What the sweep costs, by part** [M, PSP-1000/3000, cap_sweep4, the box's form A; the lab's `box_sweep_a_level_part_b30leg1every_psp1000`, `box_sweep_a_features_part_b30leg1every_psp3000`]: crowded, a sweep is ~155 µs: **the level's features (the BSP walk and the shapes made from it) ~100 µs, 65%**; the objects' half ~41 µs, 26%; the clip ~14 µs, 9%. b30's beach stages ~13-16 sweeps a tick, c20 ~33 (`gather1_features`).

**Decided, before the code:**

- **Two slices, the exact one first.** The level is read only during a tick (a broken breakable surface is its one change), so the level's half doesn't need the one-tick-old copy at all: made on the ME from the tags it gives the engine's answer, whatever the ME's timing. It's 65% of the sweep. So **slice 1 moves the level's half alone, exact** (proof: play and seeds identical, cheap), and puts the ME's hand-over, the code in eDRAM and the batch on the PSP first. **Slice 2 moves the objects' half** (26%), which reads other objects and so needs the world store's one-tick-old copy (`box_world_store.md` §9, §12): it's "plays the same", proven by the outcome census. The clip (9%) stays with the solve on the main CPU: it's the asker's own and short. That's the CLAUDE.md rule "exact stays the default where it gains about the same", applied part by part: slice 1 carries most of the gain on the cheap proof, and the copy's +1.5-2.1% a tick on the main CPU [E] (`box_world_store.md` §9) is paid only when slice 2 needs it.
- **One batch a tick, records handed as they're made** (psp_me.c's batches, `docs/me_programme.md` stage 1): a record a staged living biped, 1 KB (query in, answer out), 48 of them (48 KB). The record is added **as each biped's intent ends** (`psp_bipedstage3_intent`: the move's call is known then, kept in its intent record) and handed to the ME two at a time, so **the ME walks the level for one biped while the main CPU runs the next bipeds' intents**. Each hand-over writes back its records' lines once (psp_me_batch's `psp_me_writeback`); the ME drops the same lines from its cache before it reads them. The level's tag arrays are never written during play; at a BSP switch the ME writes back and drops its whole cache before its first record on the new level (the record names its level by its planes' address).
- **The record carries everything it reads that can change:** the level's arrays (pointers and counts, from `global_collision_bsp_get()`) and the breakable surfaces' flags (32 B) as the intents begin, and the query: the gather's exact sphere, computed by the same expressions as `psp_bipedstage2_gather_exact` (centre, radius + 1e-4, height, width, the structure flag).
- **The code in eDRAM:** MEXEC's list rbsw is rb plus `+psp_sweepme_make`, its reach by jal (the box's walk and shapes, box_collbsp.c and box_sweep.c); the C library's wrappers stay out (on the ME they take the plain routines). A batch's record function is called through a pointer, which MEXEC's copy never re-aims, so `psp_me.c`'s batch job now looks the pointer up in the copy's table on the ME (`me_mexec_aim`).
- **The level's 3D nodes in place:** the box's own copy (`box_collbsp_make`: plane inline, 32 B a node) is 1.3 MB on b30 (41,948 nodes [D], the lab's `collbsp_hot_kb_b30`), over the heap. The game's build of the box (`BOX_COLLBSP_TAG_NODES`) reads the tags' 12-byte node and its plane where they lie: two reads a step where the falsifier's form had one, so **the ME's walk here is slower than the falsifier's, by an amount only the PSP can say** [U]. A packed level form is this box's (§5 means A), and the ME's half takes it when it lands.
- **Where the main CPU collects:** the gather pass (`psp_bipedstage2_gather_exact`), biped by biped, before any solve. `psp_sweepme_find` matches the record whose query equals the gather's word for word; `psp_me_batch_need` waits for it if the ME is on it, or makes it here if the ME hasn't started it (taken back: the ME stops at that record and the main CPU makes the rest, psp_me.h's contract); the engine's gather (`collision_get_features_in_sphere`) then takes the record's leaves and features in place of its own level walk and makes the objects' half as before. After the gather pass every job is collected (`psp_me_batch_finish`), so the solves read only the gathers' own lists.
- **Missing or late:** a record not made (no room past 48 bipeds; a list cut short at the record's 32 of a kind; more than 24 leaves; features over 768 B), another level, or breakable flags changed since: the gather walks the level itself, as the old path does, counted by cause in the `sweepme:` line. A record the ME holds is waited for with psp_me_batch_need's bound (10 ms to a second look, 40 ms to stop the ME, as every batch); the record is then made here. These are the safety net; a share of records made here on the PSP is a placement to fix where it runs (the chunk, the records' order, the ME's other work), not a route back.
- **Whichever CPU makes it, the same answer:** both run `psp_sweepme_make` on the record alone (each CPU its own scratch lines), so play doesn't depend on the ME's timing: the emulator's `mebatchsimhalf` (the ring makes only half, the rest is taken back) must play identically too.
- **Effect on play: none (slice 1 is exact).** The ME's level answer is the box's restatement of the engine's walk and shapes, proven word for word on 7,371 + 12,021 + 8,946 sweeps (§9, §10); `sweepmecheck` proves it in the game on every record taken (the engine's own level half runs, the record's answer is compared with it, and the engine's is used). Inside the multiplayer boundary by being exact. Slice 2's one-tick-old objects are where play can part: there the outcome census and side-by-sides on b30_leg2, c20_leg2 and d20_play1 are its proof (`box_world_store.md` §12).
- **Budget, each unit** [U until the PSP prices it]: **ME** ~100 µs a crowded record from eDRAM at x0.86-0.94 of the main CPU's (§10), plus the in-place nodes' cost: ~1.3-1.6 ms a tick on b30's beach, ~3-3.5 ms on c20, inside the ME's room (~9 ms a tick free on the beach, `me_room_fights_*`) beside its AI, audio and drawing jobs. **Main CPU** saves the level half of each gather (~1.3-1.6 ms on b30's beach [U on M]) less the records' fill (~150 B a record), their write-back (a 1 KB record, ~11 µs an entity in halo_box's per-entity jobs, here two records a hand-over) and the take's copy (~0.2 KB). **Memory:** 48 KB of records, 2 x ~11 KB of scratch, the batch: ~70 KB of .bss in a `PSP_SWEEPME=1` build. **GE:** none.

**Slice 2, designed, not built** (the objects' half on the ME, reading the world store's one-tick-old copy):

## 12. The objects' index (builder2, 2026-11-24, design before code; the lead 12:1x)

**Why:** the objects' half of every query walks the engine's cluster lists, and a cluster holds every collideable object in it however far from the query. Counted (the lab's bpcount1, sierra-batch 45454727059 [E]; its findings rows): the broad phase meets **~2,100 objects a tick on b30, c20 and a30** (793 on d20). **Rays are the largest share** (b30 1,246, c20 942, a30 1,351), then biped motion's pill gathers (b30 653, of which 22.6 pass the packed broad phase, 3.5%; c20 1,156, 5.5%). a30's clusters hold 59-74 objects each. The same coarseness made the sweep reuse's cluster stamp too blunt (stampcen2: 0.7% of b30's repeats passed against the walk's 97%) and sets what its reach guard tests a change against (reachcen1: 376 watcher tests a tick on b30, 2,300 on c20). These are old-layer losses (the lab's REBUILD §10, #2 and the stampcen2 row): the index is the part that removes them.

**What it is:** a spatial index of the collideable objects, kept by the service and patched where an object really changes. It answers every query's objects' half ("which objects may touch this sphere / this segment") and nothing else. The level's half (the BSP walks) stays as §2-§5 have it. The engine's cluster partition stays too: perception, visibility, the drawing and the lights still walk it (`actor_perception.c`, `structure_visibility.c`, `render_objects.c`), so the index lives beside it, written at the same sites.

### 12.1 Contract

- **Reads:** each collideable root object's bounding sphere (the world store's ROOT record), and at query time only, for the candidates the index returns, the flags the queries' filters read (collision model, invisible, no collisions, a dead biped).
- **Writes:** its own cells only, at the change sites the stamps found (the lab's `tick_store_biped_plan.md`, "The stamps, read against the code"): a reconnect whose position, sphere or clusters differ from its disconnect's; a new object's connect; a deleted or newly attached object's disconnect; a pose that writes a different bounding sphere (every pose passes `object_compute_node_matrices_with`). On b30 that is ~26 changes a tick, on c20 ~94 (reachcen1's counts) [E]. An unchanged reconnect, which biped motion makes for every awake biped every tick, writes nothing.
- **Who asks:** every walk bpcount1 counted: `collision_test_point`, `collision_test_vector`, `psp_collision_test_vector_quiet`, `collision_get_features_in_sphere` and `psp_vehprobe_reach_empty`. They take the index's candidates in place of the cluster walk, then filter and test as today.
- **When:** written in the serial phase on the main CPU. A change made on the ME (point physics' ME entry) can't write main-RAM cells safely, so it is queued: a small uncached ring of (object, old sphere, new sphere) the main CPU applies at the next query or the phase's end. The stamps today mark "everything changed" instead, so how many ME changes a tick there are is an open count (12.6, item 3). Queries read only (per-query marks, §5 C), so the parallel form's queries can read the index on either core against a world that isn't moving.

### 12.2 Data

- **A loose grid in x and y** (the levels are mostly horizontal; z is the query's own test). An object lives in the one cell its sphere's center falls in. A query visits every cell within its radius plus the cell layer's largest object radius, so a move inside a cell rewrites only the object's sphere in place (no list change), and a move across cells is one unlink and one link.
- **Two layers by radius:** objects up to half a cell's size in the grid; larger ones (vehicles' and scenery's big spheres) in a short list every query tests. The split keeps a cell's loose margin small. The cell size comes from the census (12.5 step 1), not from a guess.
- **Hashed, not a full grid:** cells by (cx, cy) hashed into a fixed table (1,024 buckets [U], one 4 B head each), so the memory doesn't depend on the level's extent. A bucket collision only adds candidates the filter then drops; it never loses one.
- **Each entry 20 B, packed for the query's read:** the sphere (center, radius: 16 B, the walk's packed copy `psp_object_spheres` today), the object's 2 B absolute index, a 2 B next link. At the store's peak of ~1,121 objects a map (the lab's spec §23) that is ~22 KB, plus 4 KB of heads. It replaces `psp_object_spheres` (the broad phase's copy, ~18 KB at that peak), so the heap's net is ~+8 KB [U, before the census sizes the table]. That is a cost the lab's heap rule asks to be paid from what the store frees, per map.
- **Order:** a cell's list is in insertion order. The cluster walk's order (leaves, then each cluster's reference list, most recently connected first) isn't kept: 12.4 says why that matters and how it's proven.

### 12.3 Means

- **Work on change** (§5 B): the index is patched on the ~26-94 real changes a tick, never rebuilt per query (Form B's lesson, §8: blocks rebuilt for the level cost ~1 ms each).
- **A smaller working set** (§5 A): a query reads a few cells' packed spheres instead of every object in its clusters. For b30's pill gathers, it meets roughly the objects in reach plus a cell's margin, against 653 a tick now [U until the census counts it].
- **It serves the reuse:** the sweep reuse's reach guard watches cells in place of clusters, so a change tests only the answers kept near it (c20's 2,300 watcher tests a tick fall with the cell size [U]).

### 12.4 Proof

- **Exactness hangs on order.** The answers' feature lists are built in the order the objects are visited. If the solve's result depends on that order (the pill's plane order, the first hit at equal distances), an index visiting them differently isn't bit-exact. Then the serial form is "plays the same" within the multiplayer boundary, proven by play and the outcome census, and the multiplayer boundary's hit reports stay true (the client picks the region it saw hit). Whether order matters is measured, not assumed (12.5 step 2).
- **No object lost, by construction and by check:** every object the cluster walk would pass to the filter must be among the index's candidates. The shadow build (12.5 step 1) checks that on every query and counts any miss. One miss is a bug, not a tolerance.

### 12.5 Steps

1. **Shadow index and census** (emulator; `PSP_COLLINDEX=1`, keep word `collindexshadow`): the index built and patched beside the cluster lists, answering nothing. On every query it counts candidates, cells visited and the superset check's misses, against the walk's objects met and passed (bpcount's sites), plus histograms of query radius and object radius by layer. Exact by construction (play and seeds identical to off). It sets the cell size and the layer split. b30, c20, d20, a30.
2. **Order, measured:** a build that sorts the cluster walk's objects by absolute index, against the walk as it is, play and seeds per tick. Identical: order doesn't matter on these stretches, and the index can return candidates sorted by absolute index and be exact. Parting: the serial form is plays-the-same, and the census comes with it.
3. **Sphere gathers served from the index** (its switch on: biped pill gathers and the other sphere gathers), then **rays** (a segment stepped through the grid's cells, with the large layer tested once), then point tests and the vehicle probe. Each one proven alone against the walk, then together.
4. **The PSP pair:** old-only against the index on, b30_leg2 and c20_leg2 on the 1000, one build where the heap holds both, with its own profile recount.

### 12.6 Budget, and what's open

- **Budget:** the objects' half within the box's v0 2.2 ms a tick (§4). The cluster walk's own time was never measured [U]; at a few tens of cycles an object met and ~2,100 met a tick, plus their misses, it is roughly 0.2-0.6 ms a tick on b30 [U]. The index's target is under 0.15 ms a tick on b30's window for queries and updates together, priced on the PSP at step 4. The reuse it enables (srreach1) is priced separately.
- **Open:**
  1. Cell size and layer split, from step 1's radii.
  2. Whether the solve depends on visit order, from step 2.
  3. How many changes a tick are made on the ME (the stamps' `tick2_cs_counts[4]`, never printed): it sizes 12.1's ring.
  4. The filter's flags change without a change site (an object made invisible, a biped dying where it stands). The index doesn't need them, but the sweep reuse's reach guard does: count them before the guard leaves the census (reachcen1's 0 wrong says not on b30 and c20).
  5. Rays as long as the AI's sight lines cross many empty cells. Step 1 counts cells a ray would visit. If long rays dominate, a coarser second grid for rays is the next form.
